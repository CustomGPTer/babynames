What Baby Names does not do

Baby Names covers US and UK names only, in English. It has no community forum, no announcement cards and no AI suggestions, and it is not on Google Play, so it does not update itself. It needs Android 10 or later.

Where they do more

CharliesNames lists names from over 40 countries and learns your style. Baby Name Together has a parents' forum, polls, announcement cards and names from 40+ countries. Both install and update from Google Play.
